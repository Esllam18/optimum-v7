# Phase 4.5 QA checklist

1. Open a drawing and confirm the network board uses the full center area.
2. Confirm the title panel appears immediately to the right of the board.
3. Marquee-select several nodes, drag them together and undo.
4. Ctrl-click several nodes and use align/distribute.
5. Scale the full network without changing the frame or measured lengths.
6. Scale only a selection.
7. Zoom the board using Ctrl + wheel and confirm the surrounding web UI stays fixed.
8. Choose one node family and switch variants from its selector.
9. Choose one route family and switch route variants.
10. Edit ODF port, splitter port, core range and cable count and confirm the drawing tag updates.
11. Add up to four frame images and confirm width/height controls.
12. Export SVG, PNG and DXF and verify board/title panel separation.
13. Save and reload; confirm autosave and viewport restoration.
